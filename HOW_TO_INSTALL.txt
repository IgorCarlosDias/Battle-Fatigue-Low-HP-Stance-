====================================================================
  Battle Fatigue (Low HP Stance) v1.1.0 - How to Install
====================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------
  ENGLISH
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Option 1 — Unverum (Recommended)
1. Make sure you have Unverum installed:
   https://gamebanana.com/tools/18251
2. Download Battle-Fatigue-Low-HP-Stance-v1.1.0.zip.
3. Drag and drop the .zip into Unverum.
4. Enable Battle Fatigue (Low HP Stance) and click Launch Game.

Option 2 — Manual (UE4SS)
1. Make sure UE4SS (v3.0+) is installed in SparkingZERO\Binaries\Win64\.
2. Extract ue4ss\BattleFatigue from the .zip into:
   SparkingZERO\Binaries\Win64\Mods\BattleFatigue\
3. Launch the game (enabled.txt activates it automatically).
